What the skill asks your agent to check

Keep the useful part of tailoring.

Choose relevant work without changing the facts.

01

Give every claim a source

Start with your master resume and details you've confirmed. Mark missing information for review instead of inventing a metric. Check a public pull request's merge state before describing it as merged.

02

Write about your work

Your selected original writing can guide phrasing and rhythm through a private voice reference. Resume facts stay separate. The overlap script helps review shared wording; accurate technical terms do not need disguising.

03

Inspect the exported file

Keep the latest approved CV as the design baseline. Check extracted text and section order, compare the exported pages with that reference, and review employer autofill when offered. Parser behavior varies, so these checks cannot certify every ATS.

04

Keep personal files out of Git

Your foundation.md resume and provenance.md verification notes are excluded from version control. Your agent provider's privacy policy still applies when it reads them.

The workflow

Review before exporting.

You review the facts and wording before the agent exports a file.

  1. Choose facts and design

    Share the posting, verified sources and latest approved CV. The existing CV is the default design baseline; the agent asks before changing the look or section order.

  2. Review the draft

    Approve wording and any proposed design improvements separately. Nothing about the visual structure changes without your approval.

  3. Check extraction and export

    Verify required text and section landmarks in extracted PDF text, compare each page with the approved CV, and review employer autofill when available. These checks do not certify every ATS.

Add the skill to your agent, then review your first draft.

Local exports are tested on Linux and macOS with Python 3.11+, a Chromium-family browser, and Poppler's pdftotext and pdfinfo. GitHub CLI is used when checking public contributions.

Before you use it

Will this pass every ATS?

No. Parsers and employer configurations differ. The text-based layout and export checks help catch formatting problems, but they cannot guarantee correct parsing, a ranking or an interview.

Does this page process my resume?

No. This is a product overview with a rendered fictional sample, not a resume editor or upload form. The skill runs through your chosen agent. That provider's privacy and data policies apply to any sources you share with it.

Can it sound exactly like me?

You can now build a private style reference from selected original Markdown, Cabinet pages, Obsidian notes, Sanity exports, or normalized original-text records. It describes writing habits rather than training a model. Generated memories and assistant prose are excluded when labelled; you still confirm authorship and review the draft. Exact voice matching is not promised. Read the voice corpus guide.

Can I change the layout or paper size?

Engineering, Operations/Support, Research and Management presets reorder complete sections in one single-column layout. Roles repeat without fixed slots, and A4 or Letter is explicit. Longer CVs need an approved page limit; no content is dropped to force a pass. Inspect the fictional template gallery.

Do I need a paid resume service?

The skill is MIT licensed and does not require Rezi or Amazon Quick. You still need an agent host, and any charges or requirements of that host are separate.
